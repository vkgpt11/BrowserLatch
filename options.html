How the rules work

Websites outside the allowed list are blocked by default. Explicitly blocked domains take priority, including when a parent domain is allowed. Allowed pages may load supporting resources from other domains. Settings relock after five minutes.

The password protects these settings, but an extension cannot prevent removal from Edge. Use a child Windows account with Microsoft Family Safety or administrator-enforced Edge policies for tamper resistance.
