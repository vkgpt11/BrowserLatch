Why might sign-in or payment pages be blocked?

Pages inside an allowed website may come from a different website. Add that website to Allowed websites if you trust it and need it to open. This also lets you visit it directly.

How to keep settings protected
The password makes changes harder, but someone who can manage browser extensions can turn BrowseLatch off or remove it. For stronger protection, use a child account and browser settings managed by an adult.
